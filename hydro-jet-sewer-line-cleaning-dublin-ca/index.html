Hydro Jet Sewer Line Cleaning in Dublin, CA

Hydro jet sewer line cleaning is the use of high-pressure water, delivered through a hose with a special nozzle, to scour the inside of a sewer lateral. It differs from a cable machine, which cuts a path through a clog. If you are a Dublin, CA homeowner with a main line that keeps backing up, understanding the difference helps you ask for the right service.

This post focuses on the sewer lateral, the pipe from your house to the district connection. For drain lines inside the house, see the guide on hydro jetting for pipes in general.

What happens during a sewer jetting visit

The crew locates a cleanout, a capped pipe that gives access to the lateral. A camera goes in first to see the pipe material, the depth and any damage. The jetting hose then feeds in with a nozzle that points water backward and forward. The forward jets break through the blockage and the rear jets pull the hose through the pipe while scouring the walls.

After the pass, the camera runs again to confirm the line is clean and to record any defects that were hidden by debris.

What jetting does not do

Jetting cleans a pipe. It does not repair one. If the camera shows a crack, an offset joint or a sag, jetting may clear the line for a time but the problem returns. If a pipe is in poor condition, high pressure can make it worse, so the pressure is set to the pipe.

Tree roots are a good example. Jetting cuts them and flushes them out, but roots regrow into the same joint. A liner, a point repair or replacement fixes the joint. See sewer line repair for those options.

Signs you may need jetting

Not every backup needs it, but these patterns point that way.

Backups that return within months

The pipe wall is coated or roots are regrowing.

Slow main line drainage

Grease or scale narrows the pipe.

Gurgling in several fixtures

Partial blockage in the main.

An older cast iron lateral

Scale inside the pipe holds debris.

Aftercare for a freshly jetted line

After a jetting visit, the pipe is cleaner than it has been in years, and that is the best time to prevent the next clog. Keep grease out of the drain, use a strainer in the kitchen sink and avoid flushing wipes, even those marked flushable. Those habits keep a clean sewer line clean far longer than the cleaning alone.

If the camera pass found a belly, where the pipe sags and holds water, jetting will not fix it. Debris collects in the low spot again, and the line needs a repair to restore the slope. Ask for the recording so you can see it yourself and decide on the next step.

Questions about cost and timing

The length of the line, its access and the buildup inside it all affect the time on site. A short, easy-to-reach cleanout with moderate buildup is quicker than a long run with roots. Because of that, a plumber who quotes without seeing the access is guessing. Our sewer line cleaning page lists the factors, and a call to dispatch is the quickest way to get a number for your address.

How to prepare

Locate the cleanout and keep it accessible. Move cars and objects from the area, and tell the crew about earlier cleanings or camera footage. Do not run water during the work. After jetting, run water to test, and watch for slow drains over the next few weeks.

If you have no cleanout, ask about installing one. Cleaning through a toilet is slower and riskier than through a proper cleanout.

Dublin, CA: who runs the sewer

The Dublin San Ramon Services District provides wastewater collection and treatment for Dublin and San Ramon, and it also treats wastewater for the City of Pleasanton under contract. The property owner is generally responsible for the lateral from the house to the district connection, so ask the district where its responsibility ends before assuming a blockage is its problem. If our camera shows the blockage is beyond your property, we tell you and help you contact the district.

Questions about this topic

Is hydro jetting safe for older pipes?

On sound pipe, yes. On cracked, collapsed or very old clay pipe it can cause damage, which is why a camera comes first.

How long does it last?

That depends on what caused the buildup. Jetting a grease-coated line can give years of flow. A line with open joints and roots regrows.

Does the district clean my lateral?

Generally not. The district operates its main, and the lateral is the owner's side. Ask the district about your address.

Does jetting need a cleanout?

It is far easier with one. We can install one.
