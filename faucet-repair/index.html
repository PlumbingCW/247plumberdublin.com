Emergency faucet repair in Dublin, CA is for the faucet that will not shut off, the supply line that is spraying under the sink, or the angle stop that is stuck. A faucet issue can look small, but a failed connection under a cabinet can flood a kitchen in minutes.

Under every sink there is a pair of shutoff valves, and they are the first thing to try. Turn each clockwise to close it. If a valve will not turn or does not stop the flow, close the house main and call our 24/7 line.

Faucet emergency: stop the water first

The most common faucet emergency is a supply line that has burst or a shutoff valve that is leaking at the stem. Close the angle stops under the sink. If they do not hold, close the house main valve.

Then open the faucet to relieve pressure and put a bucket under the leak. We repair or replace the failed parts and test the connections under pressure before we leave.

Faucet problems by type

Cartridge, ball and compression faucets fail in different ways, and the repair depends on the design.

Constant drip

Worn washer, seat or cartridge. The drip wastes water every day.

Leak at the base or handle

O-rings or a loose bonnet nut.

Low flow

Mineral buildup in the aerator or a partly closed valve.

Hot and cold mixing

A failed cartridge or check stop.

Kitchen, bathroom and tub faucets

Kitchen faucets with pull-down sprayers add a hose and a weight that can fail. Bathroom faucets with pop-up drains have linkage that sticks. Tub faucets and shower valves sit inside the wall, so a leak at the handle may come from a cartridge deep in the valve.

We can repair many faucets, but if the finish is worn, the parts are discontinued or the fixture is older, a replacement is often a better value. We install faucets you bought, or help you choose one that fits your sink.

Shower and tub valve leaks are included here too. A dripping tub spout or a shower that does not shut off completely is usually a worn cartridge or a damaged seat, and replacing it prevents hidden damage in the wall.

Shutoff valves, supply lines and burst valves

Old shutoff valves are one of the most frequent hidden causes of emergency calls. They seize with age, and when you finally need them they will not close. A quarter-turn ball valve is a more reliable replacement for the multi-turn type.

Braided stainless supply lines can fail where the crimp meets the fitting, and rubber lines age. We replace both when we replace the faucet. A burst or broken supply valve is the situation where having a plumber with the parts on the truck matters most.

Dublin water and faucet wear

Dublin water is supplied by the Dublin San Ramon Services District, which buys from Zone 7 Water Agency. Zone 7 says its groundwater is over 300 mg/L hardness and its surface water is under 100 mg/L, so the tap can run hard at times. Hard water leaves scale on aerators and cartridges, which reduces flow and wears seals.

Cleaning aerators every few months and replacing cartridges as needed keeps faucets working. If you see white crust on fixtures, test your tap or ask the district for its water quality report.

Codes and permits

For faucet repair, the plumber works to the California Plumbing Code, which the state bases on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). If your project replaces or moves pipes, valves or fixtures, check with the City of Dublin before work starts, because that kind of work is not on its exempt list.

Questions we hear in Dublin

Do you handle faucet emergencies at night or on weekends in Dublin?

Yes. Our line is staffed 24/7. If a supply line or valve is leaking, close the stop under the sink, or the main, and call.

My shutoff valve will not turn. What do I do?

Do not force it, since the stem can break. Close the house main instead and call. We replace the valve so it works next time.

Is it worth repairing an old faucet?

If parts are available and the faucet is sound, yes. If it is worn, discontinued or leaking from several places, replacement is often better.

Can you install a faucet I bought?

Yes, if it fits the sink and the supply lines. We replace the connectors and shutoffs when needed.
