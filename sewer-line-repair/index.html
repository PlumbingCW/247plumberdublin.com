Emergency sewer line repair in Dublin, CA is for the lateral that runs from your house to the connection at the district main. When it breaks, shifts, fills with roots or collapses, wastewater backs up into the lowest drains of the house, and the first call should be to a plumber who can camera the line the same day.

Stop using water when a sewer backup starts. Do not flush, run the washer or take a shower, because every gallon goes to the lowest opening in the house. Keep family and pets away from affected floors and call our 24/7 dispatch.

Sewer backup emergency: what to do first

A true sewer backup is a health hazard, because the water is contaminated. Keep people off the wet area, open windows, and do not use the plumbing. If the backup came through a floor drain, shower or tub, the main line is blocked or broken below it.

We arrive with cable machines and a camera. Clearing the line gets you running again, and the camera shows why it blocked, which decides whether you need a one-time repair, a liner or a replacement.

What causes a sewer lateral to fail

Tree roots seek moisture and enter through joints. Cast iron and clay pipes corrode or crack with age, and shifting soil can offset a joint so waste catches on the lip. Grease and flushed wipes then build up in the damaged spot, and each blockage starts the cycle again.

Dublin's sewer service comes from the Dublin San Ramon Services District, which treats wastewater for Dublin and San Ramon. The district operates the main, while the lateral from your house to the connection is generally the property owner's side. Ask the district where its responsibility ends, because it decides who pays.

Homes built since the 1980s often have plastic laterals, which resist roots better but can still sag or crack. Older homes from the 1960s and 1970s may have clay or cast iron. Age is not a diagnosis, so we confirm with a camera.

Signs the problem is the main sewer line

One slow drain is rarely the sewer. These patterns usually are.

Multiple drains slow at once

All of them share the main, so a main blockage slows the whole house.

Gurgling in toilets or tubs

Air is displaced when water moves in a partly blocked line.

Wet or sunken patch in the yard

A lawn that stays soggy along the sewer path can mean a break.

Repeated backups after cabling

A cable only clears the clog, and the root cause remains.

Pipe lining and trenchless repair, in plain terms

A cured-in-place liner is a resin-soaked tube pulled into the old pipe and inflated until it hardens, forming a pipe inside the pipe. It works when the host pipe is intact enough to hold its shape and can be cleaned first. It does not fix a collapsed or deeply sagging section.

Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one and breaks the old pipe outward. It needs a pit at each end and a clear path. Trenchless methods save lawns, driveways and mature plants, but they are not always available, and a camera tells us which applies.

Permits, utilities and underground work

Sewer lateral work involves digging and often a permit. Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Dublin Building and Safety Division what is required for your project. Before any digging, a call to 811 for underground utility locating is standard practice, and we plan that into the schedule.

Landmarks where we get called include neighborhoods around Emerald Glen Park, Fallon Sports Park and the older streets close to Dublin Boulevard. We also run sewer repairs in Pleasanton and San Ramon.

Cast iron, clay and plastic sewer pipes

Older sewer laterals may be cast iron or clay, and newer ones are usually plastic. Cast iron rusts from the inside and scales, which narrows the pipe and catches debris. Clay is made of short sections, and roots can enter at the joints.

A camera shows which material you have and where it is failing. That decides whether a spot repair, a liner or a full replacement makes sense, and we explain the trade-offs before work starts.

Codes and permits

Plumbing in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) published by IAPMO. Our work on sewer line repair is planned with that code in mind, and the City of Dublin decides when a permit is needed.

Questions we hear in Dublin

Do you handle sewer emergencies at night or on weekends in Dublin?

Yes. Our 24/7 dispatch covers sewer backups at any hour. Stop using water and call. We can clear the line and camera it to find the cause.

How do I know if my sewer line needs replacement?

Repeated backups, visible cracks or offsets on the camera, collapsed sections and root intrusion at several joints all point to replacement or lining. A single root clog in an otherwise healthy line usually does not.

Who pays for a sewer lateral repair?

Generally the property owner is responsible for the lateral from the house to the district connection. Confirm with the Dublin San Ramon Services District where its responsibility starts for your address.

Can the sewer line be lined without digging?

Often yes, if the pipe is continuous and round enough. A camera inspection decides. A collapsed section usually requires digging or bursting.
