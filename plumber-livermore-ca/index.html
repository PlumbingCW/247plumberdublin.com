Dry summers and the leaks they hide

Livermore's long, dry summers put a load on irrigation and exterior plumbing that Dublin homeowners with small lots may not feel as strongly. Lawn zones, drip lines and hose bibs run for hours, and a failing valve or a buried fitting can leak for weeks without any visible puddle. The first sign is often a high bill or a patch of green in a dry yard.

We start those calls with a meter test and a look at the irrigation controller. Our leak detection page explains how we separate an irrigation leak from a service line leak, and water service line repair covers the pipe between the meter and the house.

Water heaters in garages also work harder in cold mornings and run in hot ones. A tank heater that fails in August or in January is one more reason Livermore homeowners call for water heater repair.

If a line breaks or water is spreading in Livermore

Close the main valve at the house first. If the leak is outdoors and the house valve does not stop it, the break is upstream, between the meter and the house, and the meter box valve belongs to your water provider. Do not force a meter valve. Call us and your provider.

Water hardness: east side of the Tri-Valley

Zone 7 Water Agency says its western areas are generally harder than its eastern ones, and that its groundwater is over 300 mg/L in hardness while its surface water is under 100 mg/L. Livermore is at the eastern end, so the mix at your tap may differ from what a homeowner in western Dublin sees. We do not guess a number for your address.

If you are considering a softener, test your tap and ask your provider for its annual water quality report. Our water softener and filtration page lays out the options and the Zone 7 cautions about sodium and salt discharge.

Codes and permits in Livermore

Plumbing work in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) from IAPMO. Permit rules are set by each city, so a job outside Dublin follows the local building department, and our plumber tells you before work begins if a permit applies.
