Written by the Dublin 24/7 Plumbing team. Updated November 16, 2026.

Emergency toilet repair in Dublin, CA starts at the shutoff valve behind the bowl. Turn it clockwise to stop the supply, then call our 24/7 line if the toilet is overflowing, leaking onto the floor or will not stop running. Most toilet problems are fixed in a single visit, whether that means a new flapper or a new toilet.

Toilet installation is the other half of this page. A new toilet needs a flat flange, a fresh wax ring or gasket, and a shutoff valve that works, and getting those right avoids leaks under the floor.

Toilet overflow emergency: the first minute

An overflowing toilet is a stoppage, not a mechanical failure, and the water is contaminated. Reach behind the bowl and close the supply valve, then lift the tank lid and push the flapper down to stop more water entering the bowl.

Do not flush again. Put towels at the base to slow the spread, and call us. If several toilets and tubs are acting up at once, the blockage may be in the main line, which we cover on the drain cleaning page.

Common toilet problems and what causes them

Most toilet calls trace back to a few parts. Here are the ones we see most.

Constantly running

A flapper that does not seal, a fill valve that leaks, or a float set too high. Often a ten-minute repair.

Weak flush

Clogged rim jets, a low tank water level or a partial clog downstream.

Rocking or leaking at the base

A failed wax ring or a loose closet bolt. Water here can damage the subfloor if ignored.

Phantom flushing

Tank water slowly leaks into the bowl, and the fill valve refills it on a cycle.

Wax rings, flanges and why the base leaks

A wax ring or modern gasket seals the connection between the toilet horn and the floor flange. If the toilet is not set evenly, if the flange is too low, or if a loose bolt lets the toilet rock, the seal breaks and leaks seep under the bowl.

Check for a leak by looking at the caulk line and at the floor around the base. Soft flooring, a musty smell or discolored vinyl near the toilet are warning signs. We pull the toilet, inspect the flange and subfloor, replace the ring, and reset the toilet level.

If the flange is cracked or sits below the finished floor, we repair or extend it so the new seal holds. We never reuse an old wax ring on a toilet that has been moved.

Installing a new toilet

Installation is more than swapping a bowl. We confirm the rough-in distance from the wall to the flange center, since 12 inches is the most common but not universal. We check that the flange is secure and level, install a new supply line and shutoff valve if the old ones are corroded, and set the toilet with a new seal.

Height, bowl shape and flush type matter. Comfort-height toilets are taller than standard bowls, elongated bowls are longer than round ones, and a dual-flush model uses less water. We help you choose one that fits the space and the budget, and we can install a toilet you bought yourself.

Some jobs need permits. Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Dublin Building and Safety Division if your project moves plumbing. A same-location toilet replacement generally does not, but confirm before relying on that.

Dublin homes, water and toilets

Dublin's water comes through the Dublin San Ramon Services District, which buys wholesale water from Zone 7 Water Agency. Zone 7 says its water is a blend of surface water and groundwater, and the groundwater is harder, which can leave mineral deposits that clog rim jets and foul fill valves over time.

If your toilet has a weak flush with no clog, mineral deposits in the rim jets are a likely cause, and a careful cleaning often restores the flush. Replacing the fill valve every several years is a cheap way to keep a toilet reliable.

What to do while a toilet overflows

An overflowing toilet is messy but usually quick to stop.

Stop the flow

Lift the tank lid and push the flapper down, or close the supply valve behind the toilet by turning it clockwise.

Do not flush again

Another flush adds water to a bowl that cannot drain.

Clean safely

Wear gloves, soak up water with towels and disinfect the floor.

Call if it repeats

Repeated clogs can mean a blocked drain line, which needs a camera or a cable.

Codes and permits

The California Plumbing Code is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and it sets the baseline for toilet repair & installation in Dublin. Clearing a stoppage or repairing a leak is on the City of Dublin permit-exempt list, while replacing fixtures or rearranging pipes is not, so ask us whether your job needs one.

Questions we hear in Dublin

Do you handle toilet emergencies at night or on weekends in Dublin?

Yes. Our line is staffed 24/7. Close the supply valve and call, and we send a plumber for overflows, leaks and constant running.

Why does my toilet keep running?

Usually a worn flapper or a fill valve that does not shut off. A few drops of food coloring in the tank will show whether the flapper leaks into the bowl.

How much clearance does a toilet need?

A common rule is about 15 inches from the center of the toilet to a side wall or fixture, and the rough-in is typically 12 inches from the wall. Confirm yours before buying a toilet.

Can you install a toilet I bought myself?

Yes, as long as it fits the rough-in and is in good condition. We provide the labor and the parts that are needed, such as the seal and supply line.
