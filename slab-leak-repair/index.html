Emergency slab leak repair in Dublin, CA starts with confirming the leak is under the concrete, not in a wall or the yard. A slab leak is water escaping from a pipe under or inside a concrete foundation, and the signs are a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water, damp carpet or a meter that keeps turning with every fixture off.

Shut off the main water valve and call. If the floor is hot, a hot-water line is likely leaking and the water heater may be running more than usual, so close the heater's cold supply too.

Why a slab leak is an emergency

Water under a slab does not stay put. It can soften the soil, damage flooring and promote mold, and a slab leak on a hot-water line wastes energy every hour it runs. Because the pipe is hidden, the bill often rises before anyone sees a symptom.

We treat a suspected slab leak as same-day when we can. A shutoff stops the flow, and the electronic locating that follows usually finds the leak within a few hours.

What causes a slab leak

Pipes under slabs fail from corrosion, abrasion against concrete or gravel, and settling that stresses joints. Copper pipe can develop pinholes from water chemistry and from friction as it expands and contracts. In some homes the pipe was installed in a sleeve, and a sleeve that has failed lets water pool around the line.

Dublin's homes are mostly newer than those in many Bay Area cities, but not all have the same plumbing. Slab-on-grade homes are common in the Tri-Valley, and the supply and drain lines under them were installed in different decades with different materials. A camera, pressure test and leak locator tell us what applies to your house.

Slab leak repair options

There are three common paths, and the right one depends on the pipe, the number of leaks and your budget.

Option.How it works.When it fits.
Spot repair through the slab.Open a small hole in the concrete, repair the pipe, and patch the slab.One accessible leak on an otherwise sound pipe.
Reroute through walls or attic.Abandon the leaking line and run new pipe overhead.Leak is hard to reach or the line is old.
Repipe.Replace the supply lines in the house.Repeated leaks or a failing material.

Flooring, insurance and what happens after the repair

Spot repairs through the slab affect flooring directly. Tile and wood may need to be cut, and matching can be difficult. Rerouting avoids that and leaves the floor as is. We tell you what each option means for your floor before you choose.

Many policies cover water damage from a sudden leak and the cost of tearing out to access it, but not always the pipe itself. Photograph the area and the meter before cleanup, and ask your insurer about coverage. We provide a written description of the leak and the repair.

Dublin water pressure, meters and slab leaks

In Dublin, the Dublin San Ramon Services District provides water and owns the meter. Pressure varies by street, and homes with high pressure see more stress on slab pipes. A pressure gauge on a hose bib takes a minute and tells you whether a regulator needs attention.

The district can provide usage history. A rise in winter usage with no change in household is a stronger clue than a rise in summer. If the leak is on the house side of the meter, it is the owner's repair, and we can document the finding. We serve homes across Dublin, from the streets near Emerald Glen Park to those close to Camp Parks, and in Pleasanton and San Ramon.

Codes and permits

Plumbing in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) published by IAPMO. Our work on slab leak repair is planned with that code in mind, and the City of Dublin decides when a permit is needed.

Questions we hear in Dublin

Do you handle slab leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Dublin?

Yes. Dispatch is open 24/7. Shut the main valve and call. We send a plumber to start the diagnosis, and a locator crew when needed.

How do I know if I have a slab leak?

Warm spots on the floor, running water sounds when nothing is on, damp carpet, cracks in flooring and a meter that moves with everything off. The meter test is the fastest check.

Do you have to break the concrete?

Not always. Rerouting avoids concrete work. Spot repairs need a small opening. We explain the options before any cutting.

Is a slab leak covered by insurance?

Coverage varies. Many policies cover the resulting water damage and access costs, but not the pipe. Ask your insurer, and keep our written report.
