If a sink, tub or floor drain in your Dublin, CA home has stopped draining, you have an emergency drain cleaning job on your hands, and our 24/7 line is the fastest way to get a plumber moving. Drain cleaning means clearing the blockage with a hand auger, a motorized cable machine or water pressure, then confirming the line flows freely.

While you wait, stop running water into the blocked fixture, keep the garbage disposal off if the sink is involved, and move rugs and electronics away from any floor drain that is gurgling. If several fixtures back up at once, the problem is probably in the main line, so do not keep flushing toilets.

When a clogged drain is an emergency

Most clogs are a nuisance. A drain becomes an emergency when water is rising toward the rim, when sewage smell fills the room, or when a floor drain or tub starts bubbling as you run something elsewhere. Those are signs that the blockage is deeper than the fixture, and every extra flush adds to the mess.

We treat a full backup, an overflow risk or a house with one working bathroom as same-day, after-hours work. A single slow bathroom sink can usually wait for a scheduled visit, and we will tell you which category yours is when you call.

Kitchen sink clogs: grease, food and disposals

Kitchen drains clog differently from bathrooms. Cooking grease cools inside the pipe and collects food scraps and soap film, so the narrowing happens a little more every week until a bad night finishes the job. Garbage disposals grind scraps into a paste that lines the trap arm, and fibrous waste such as celery, onion skin and potato peels wraps around itself.

For a kitchen sink we start at the trap and the horizontal arm, then run a small cable toward the wall tee and, if needed, further to the stack. If the cable keeps returning with grease, we recommend a hydro jetting pass because a cable only bores a hole through the middle of that buildup.

Dishwashers often drain through the disposal or the same trap, so a standing pool in the dishwasher after a cycle is part of the same diagnosis. We check that connection before we leave.

Bathroom, tub and shower drains

Hair and soap scum are the usual causes upstairs. A tub that drains slowly but a sink that drains fine points to a clog in that fixture's own trap. If the tub and the toilet on the same floor are both slow, the shared branch line is partly blocked.

Slow tub or shower

Usually hair and soap film in the trap or the first few feet of pipe. A hand auger clears most of these without removing fixtures.

Gurgling after a flush

Air is being pulled through a partly blocked branch or vent. Ignored, it often becomes a full stoppage.

Toilet and tub both slow

The shared branch line is restricted. This needs a cable machine, not a plunger.

Water backing up in the tub when the washer drains

The main line is blocked below those fixtures. Stop using water and call.

Main line stoppages and what they cost in time

A main sewer line carries everything from the house to the connection at the street. Roots, grease, wipes labeled flushable and bellied sections of pipe all cause stoppages. The first sign is often the lowest drain in the house, such as a shower or floor drain, rising when water is used upstairs.

Dublin sewer service is provided by the Dublin San Ramon Services District, which treats wastewater for both Dublin and San Ramon. The district's responsibility generally ends at its main, and the lateral from your house to that connection is typically the property owner's side, so ask the district where the line of responsibility sits before you assume a blockage is a city problem.

If the blockage is in the lateral, we clear it with a cable machine and recommend a camera pass so you can see roots, a crack or an offset joint instead of guessing. Our sewer camera inspection page explains what the footage shows, and sewer line cleaning covers the heavier equipment.

Snaking, cabling and what gets recommended next

Drain snaking uses a coiled steel cable that turns as it feeds into the pipe. The cutting head either hooks the clog and pulls it out or breaks it apart so water can carry it away. We match the cable size to the pipe: a small cable for a bathroom sink, a larger machine for a four-inch main.

After clearing, we run water to confirm full flow and look at what came out. Hair points to a drain strainer habit, grease points to a kitchen routine, and tree roots point to a sewer lateral that needs a camera. You get a plain description of the cause and the options, with the price stated before more work begins.

If a line has been cleared twice in a year, we will usually recommend a camera look instead of a third visit with a cable. Repeat stoppages are almost always a symptom of something physical in the pipe.

Dublin homes and the drains we see

Dublin was a small town of a few hundred people in 1960 and had nearly 14,000 residents when it incorporated in 1982, according to the City of Dublin, and it has grown to more than 65,000 since.

That means homes in the city span several building eras. Houses from the 1960s and 1970s may still have cast iron drain lines that scale inside and catch debris, while homes built since the 1980s often have plastic drain pipe. We ask the age of the house on every drain call because it changes what we expect to find.

Apartments and condos, including the buildings around the Dublin/Pleasanton BART station, often share a drain stack, so a blockage can affect neighbors. Tell the dispatcher if you share a stack and we will plan for it.

Codes and permits

Plumbing in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) published by IAPMO. Our work on drain cleaning is planned with that code in mind, and the City of Dublin decides when a permit is needed.

Questions we hear in Dublin

Do you handle drain emergencies at night or on weekends in Dublin?

Yes. Dispatch runs 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Call, describe which drains are affected, and we send a plumber with the right machine. After-hours visits carry a different rate than daytime work, and we state the price before work starts.

Is it safe to use chemical drain cleaner first?

We advise against it. Caustic cleaners can sit in a fully blocked pipe and burn skin when the pipe is later opened, and they can damage older metal fittings. A plunger or a hand auger is the safer first try, and a plumber can clear the line without exposing anyone to the chemicals.

Why does my kitchen drain keep clogging?

Grease and food paste coat the pipe wall, so each cable pass only opens a narrow channel. A hydro jetting pass cleans the full wall. A camera can also show whether a sag in the line is holding water and debris.

How do I know if the clog is in my line or in the district main?

If only one fixture is slow, the clog is almost certainly inside your house. If every drain is slow and the lowest drains back up, the blockage is in the lateral or the main. Ask the Dublin San Ramon Services District where its responsibility ends, and we can camera the line to find where the problem sits.
