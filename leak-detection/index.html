Written by the Dublin 24/7 Plumbing team. Updated November 16, 2026.

Emergency leak detection in Dublin, CA means finding where water is escaping before it ruins drywall, flooring or a slab. We start with the symptoms you can see, such as a spinning meter, a surprise water bill or a damp ceiling, and use listening equipment, pressure tests and thermal imaging to pinpoint the pipe.

If water is actively flowing, close the main shutoff valve at the house, then call our 24/7 line. If the meter keeps moving with every fixture off, you have a leak somewhere between the meter and the house, inside a wall or under the floor.

When a leak is an emergency

A hidden leak becomes urgent when water is stained through a ceiling, when you hear water running in a wall with everything off, or when the floor feels warm or damp over a slab. Water follows framing and pipes, so the wet spot you see is often far from the source.

Closing the main valve stops the flow and buys time. We then trace the leak by process of elimination, and the shutoff test below usually narrows the zone within minutes.

The two-hour meter test you can do before we arrive

This simple check separates a leak outside the house from one inside it. It costs nothing, and the numbers help us decide where to look first.

1.

Turn everything off.

Close taps, stop the washer and dishwasher, and pause the ice maker.

2.

Read the meter.

Note the dial and the small leak indicator, a triangle or wheel that spins with any flow.

3.

Wait two hours.

Use no water during that time.

4.

Read again.

Any change means water is escaping somewhere on your side of the meter.

High water bill: finding the source

A bill that jumps without a lifestyle change is one of the most common reasons people call. Toilets, irrigation, and supply lines are the first suspects.

Running toilet

A flapper that does not seal can pass hundreds of gallons a day with almost no sound. A few drops of food coloring in the tank show it in minutes.

Irrigation valve stuck open

Sprinkler zones that never fully close raise usage, especially if the meter spins at night.

Leak between meter and house

The service line is under the yard, so the signs are a soggy patch, a pressure drop or a meter that never stops.

Slow pinhole in a wall

Copper pinholes drip inside the wall for months and raise the bill before any stain appears.

How we find a leak without tearing out a wall

Acoustic listening equipment picks up the sound of pressurized water escaping through a small opening, and it works on both copper and plastic when the pipe is under pressure. We use ground microphones along a service line and contact sensors on walls and floors.

Thermal imaging shows temperature differences on surfaces. A hot water line leaking under a floor shows as a warm trail, and a cold line can show as a cool patch. A moisture meter then confirms whether the material is wet, because a stain can be old and dry.

For a leak in a closed system, we isolate sections and use pressure gauges to see which section loses pressure. Gas, tracer or camera methods come last. Once the location is known, we can often open a single small access hole instead of removing entire sections.

Ceiling, wall and attic leaks

A ceiling stain is usually a drain line, a supply line or a roof leak. A hot line leak and a roof leak look similar until you test them. We check whether the stain grows when it rains, when a particular fixture runs, or when the water heater cycles.

Wall leaks often come from supply lines behind showers, valves that have loosened, or old pinholes. We cut a neat inspection hole, repair the pipe, and leave drywall ready for patching. Water damage cleanup and drywall repair are separate trades, so we point you to a restoration company when that is needed.

Under-house and crawlspace leaks

Some Dublin homes have raised foundations with crawlspaces, and the pipes there can leak for a long time without anyone noticing. Signs include a musty smell, a damp subfloor, warped hardwood and a floor that feels soft near a bathroom.

A crawlspace leak is often easier to repair than a slab leak because the pipe is reachable. We check supply lines, drain connections and the tub trap, then recommend a repair or a reroute. If the pipe type is the problem, such as aging galvanized or polybutylene, we explain the repiping option so you can compare it to repeated spot repairs.

Yard and meter box leaks, and slab leaks

Yard leaks sit between the meter and the house. The district owns the meter, and the pipe after it is generally the homeowner's, so the repair is yours. A wet, green patch in dry weather, a lawn dip or a sudden drop in pressure all point there.

When the floor is warm, the sound of water runs under the slab, or the meter runs with the house off, a slab leak is possible. That is its own topic, covered on our slab leak repair page, and a leak in the yard service line is covered under water service line repair.

Irrigation leaks are common too. Zone valves, risers and drip lines are cheaper to fix than a service line, so we test those before we dig. We also handle pool-equipment plumbing leaks when the pool equipment connections are the issue.

Dublin water, the meter and your bill

In Dublin, the Dublin San Ramon Services District provides drinking water and wastewater service, so your bill, the meter and the district's rules all come from the same agency. The district can tell you your usage history, which helps spot when a leak started. Leaks on the house side of the meter are the property owner's responsibility.

If you are checking a bill, compare the same months across years, because summer irrigation raises usage. A flat increase in winter usage with no change in occupants is a much stronger sign of a hidden leak than a spike in July. We are glad to look at the pattern with you.

Codes and permits

For leak detection, the plumber works to the California Plumbing Code, which the state bases on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). If your project replaces or moves pipes, valves or fixtures, check with the City of Dublin before work starts, because that kind of work is not on its exempt list.

Questions we hear in Dublin

Do you handle leak emergencies at night or on weekends in Dublin?

Yes, the line is staffed 24/7. If water is flowing, shut the main valve first, then call. We send a plumber with the equipment to locate the leak and stop the damage.

How accurate is electronic leak detection?

It is accurate when pressure is on the line and the conditions allow listening, but no method is perfect. We combine more than one method, confirm with a moisture reading, and open the smallest access we can.

Does a high water bill always mean a leak?

No. Irrigation, a new landscape, guests, a pool refill and seasonal use all raise usage. A leak is likely when usage rises with no explanation, and the meter test above is the quickest way to confirm.

Is a leak behind the meter my responsibility?

Generally the district owns the meter, and the pipe from the meter to the house is the property owner's. Confirm with the Dublin San Ramon Services District where its responsibility ends for your address.
