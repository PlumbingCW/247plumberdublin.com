Restaurants, grease and drain maintenance

Commercial kitchens produce grease continually. A grease interceptor captures some of it, and it needs regular pumping by a licensed hauler. Lines downstream of the interceptor still build up, so scheduled jetting and camera checks prevent a backup in the middle of a Friday service.

We offer drain cleaning and hydro jetting for commercial lines, and we advise on schedule based on what the camera shows. Pumping and hauling grease from interceptors is a separate service done by haulers, and we coordinate with them. See hydro jetting for the method.

Backflow testing and compliance

Commercial properties often have backflow preventers on the water service or irrigation. These are assemblies that stop used water from flowing backward into the potable supply, and many require annual testing by a certified tester under the local water district's rules.

Ask the Dublin San Ramon Services District which devices on your property need testing and how often. The district sets the testing program for its customers. We can coordinate testing as part of scheduled maintenance and repairs, and report any failures we find in the assembly.

What to do before we arrive

If water is spreading through a shop, restaurant or office, these steps limit the damage while a plumber is on the way.

Close the valve

Shut the main or the valve for the affected branch if you can reach it.

Protect people

Keep staff and customers away from wet floors and put out a sign.

Mind the power

Do not touch outlets or equipment standing in water. Turn off power only if the panel is dry and safe to reach.

Document the damage

Take photos before cleanup for your records and for any insurance claim.

Backflow preventers on commercial properties

Many commercial sites have a backflow preventer on a fire line, an irrigation system or the main service. The device stops water from flowing backward into the public supply. It is a mechanical part that can fail, leak or freeze-damage, and it needs periodic testing.

Testing rules come from the water supplier. For Dublin, that is the Dublin San Ramon Services District, so ask the district what it requires for your property and how often. We repair and replace preventers and can plan the work around your business hours.

Codes and permits

The California Plumbing Code is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and it sets the baseline for commercial plumbing in Dublin. Clearing a stoppage or repairing a leak is on the City of Dublin permit-exempt list, while replacing fixtures or rearranging pipes is not, so ask us whether your job needs one.
