Emergency sewer line cleaning in Dublin, CA is for the blockage in the main line that carries all the wastewater from your house to the district connection. Signs include several slow drains, gurgling toilets and water coming up in the lowest drain, and the faster you stop using water the less mess there is.

Find the cleanout, a capped pipe in the yard or at the side of the house, and keep it accessible. If sewage is already rising, leave the area, avoid contact and call 24/7 dispatch.

Main line blocked tonight: a sewer emergency

A main sewer blockage is a health risk and cannot wait. Water, soap and waste have nowhere to go, and the lowest fixture in the house takes the overflow. A shower, tub or floor drain is usually the first to rise.

We arrive with a cable machine sized for four-inch lines, and often a jetter. The goal is to open the line quickly, then find out why it blocked.

Main line clearing: what happens on site

We locate the cleanout and feed a cable machine with a cutting head into the line. The head cuts roots, breaks up grease and pushes through the blockage. If the cleanout is missing, we may enter through a toilet or a roof vent, and we recommend installing a cleanout so future work is faster.

Once water flows, we run a camera through the line. This shows whether the clog was a one-time event or a symptom of a damaged pipe. Footage also shows where roots entered, how much grease has built up and whether there are sags, cracks or offset joints.

If the line is damaged, we discuss repair. If the line is healthy and buildup is the cause, we recommend a jetting pass and give habits to reduce buildup.

Why main lines clog

Most main line stoppages come from one of four causes.

Tree roots

They enter joints and grow into a dense mat that catches paper and grease.

Grease and fat

Cools and hardens on the pipe wall over months.

Flushed wipes and foreign objects

Wipes labeled flushable do not break down like toilet paper.

Sags and offsets

Low spots hold water and debris, causing repeat clogs.

Cleanouts, locating and access

A cleanout is a capped access point on the lateral. Without one, cleaning is slower and may need a toilet removed. Many homes have one near the foundation or at the property line, and if yours is buried, we can find it with a locator. We can also install a new cleanout, which is a small job compared with repeated access problems.

The district main is the district's responsibility. The lateral from the house to the connection is generally yours. Ask the Dublin San Ramon Services District where its responsibility ends. If the blockage is in the district's main, it is not a repair you pay for, and we can help you report it.

Dublin: wastewater, wipes and what causes backups

The Dublin San Ramon Services District collects and treats wastewater for Dublin and San Ramon. Wipes and grease are two of the most common causes of lateral and main blockages, and even products labeled flushable belong in the trash rather than the toilet.

Large apartment and condo buildings near the Dublin/Pleasanton BART station and along Dublin Boulevard have shared building lines, and commercial kitchens add grease. We clean lines in all of these settings, and in Pleasanton and Danville too.

What to do before we arrive

A sewer backup carries bacteria, so treat it with care until the line is cleared.

Stop using water

Do not run sinks, showers, washers or dishwashers while the line is blocked.

Avoid contact

Keep children and pets away from any backup. Wear gloves and boots if you must enter the area.

Ventilate

Open windows if the smell is strong and the weather allows.

Do not use chemicals

Drain chemicals can splash back and can harm the plumber and the pipe.

Storm drains are a separate system

A storm drain carries rainwater and is separate from the sanitary sewer that serves your toilets and sinks. Only rain should enter it, so never pour cleaning water, paint, oil or sewage into a yard or street drain.

Who clears a storm drain depends on where it is. A drain in the street is handled by the city, so call the City of Dublin public works with its location. A drain on your own property, such as a yard or driveway drain, is yours to maintain, and we can clear it with a cable or a jetter and check where it leads.

Codes and permits

Plumbing in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) published by IAPMO. Our work on sewer line cleaning is planned with that code in mind, and the City of Dublin decides when a permit is needed.

Questions we hear in Dublin

Do you clean sewer lines at night or on weekends in Dublin?

Yes. Dispatch is 24/7 for sewer backups. Stop using water and call.

What is a sewer cleanout?

A capped pipe that gives access to the sewer lateral. It makes cleaning and camera work faster and cheaper.

How often should a sewer line be cleaned?

Only as needed. A line with a history of roots or grease may need periodic cleaning. A camera shows whether it is worth scheduling.

Will you tell me if the problem is the district main?

Yes. If a camera shows the blockage is beyond your property, we tell you and help you contact the Dublin San Ramon Services District.
