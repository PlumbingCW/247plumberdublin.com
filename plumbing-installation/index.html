Written by the Dublin 24/7 Plumbing team. Updated November 16, 2026.

Plumbing installation in Dublin, CA covers new work: adding a bathroom or a laundry, moving a sink during a remodel, installing a dishwasher or an ice maker line, adding shutoff valves and installing fixtures such as sinks, tubs and showers. Planning comes first, because the cost of moving a drain is mostly a matter of where it sits.

For repairs to existing fixtures, see faucet repair or toilet repair and installation. This page is for planned installs and remodel plumbing.

Remodel and addition plumbing

New plumbing has two stages. Rough-in installs drain, vent and supply lines inside open walls or floors, and finish work installs fixtures after surfaces are done. Both stages usually need inspection by the city.

We coordinate with your contractor on timing so walls are not closed before inspection. For small projects, such as moving a kitchen sink a few feet, we describe what changes and why, and give you a written price.

Permit rules vary by project, so ask the City of Dublin Building and Safety Division. The City's handout lists work that is exempt from permit and notes that replacing or rearranging pipes, valves, fittings or fixtures is not exempt, so most installation projects will need a permit.

Shutoffs, regulators and protection devices

An install is a good moment to improve the system. Replacing old multi-turn valves with quarter-turn ball valves makes shutoffs reliable. A pressure regulator protects fixtures if the incoming pressure is high. An expansion tank protects a closed system from thermal expansion.

Backflow prevention applies to irrigation, fire lines and some commercial equipment. Your water district may require testing of installed devices. Ask the Dublin San Ramon Services District which rules apply to your property.

Dublin remodels and new construction

Dublin has grown steadily since incorporation in 1982, and homes of every era get remodeled. Whenever a kitchen, bathroom or laundry layout changes, the drain and vent routes need to be planned ahead of time. New construction plumbing is a different trade and is handled by the builder's plumber.

We also serve Livermore and San Ramon for planned installs.

Backflow prevention and your fixtures

Some fixtures and connections need a device that stops water from flowing backward. An outdoor hose bib can have a vacuum breaker, an irrigation system often has a backflow preventer, and a commercial or fire line may need a larger assembly.

We install and replace these devices as part of a plumbing job. If your property needs periodic testing, the water supplier sets the rules, and for Dublin that is the Dublin San Ramon Services District.

Codes and permits

For plumbing installation, the plumber works to the California Plumbing Code, which the state bases on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). If your project replaces or moves pipes, valves or fixtures, check with the City of Dublin before work starts, because that kind of work is not on its exempt list.

Questions we hear in Dublin

Do I need a permit for plumbing installation in Dublin?

Permit rules vary by project. The City of Dublin Building and Safety Division can tell you what applies. Replacing or rearranging pipes and fixtures is generally not exempt.

Can you install fixtures I bought?

Yes. We confirm they fit and replace supply lines and shutoffs as needed.

Can you work with my contractor?

Yes. We coordinate rough-in and finish timing.

Do you install gas lines?

Gas line work is a separate scope. Ask when you call and we tell you whether we can help.
