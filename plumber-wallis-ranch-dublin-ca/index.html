Plumber in Wallis Ranch, Dublin, CA

Wallis Ranch is a master-planned community of newer homes off Tassajara Road in east Dublin. This page covers what public sources document and the plumbing questions that homes under ten years old raise.

Where Wallis Ranch is

Wallis Ranch is in east Dublin. A 2014 equity announcement describes the 184-acre site as lying north of Roxbury on Tassajara Road and extending uphill to the Alameda and Contra Costa county line. The City of Dublin lists Wallis Ranch Park at 6701 Rutherford Road, which is a useful anchor.

Tassajara Road continues north into Contra Costa County, where we also serve Danville as a separate town. No public source we found gives lot-level boundaries for the eight neighborhoods inside Wallis Ranch.

What is documented about the homes

The 2014 announcement says the project was approved for 805 single-family homes, while Wikipedia gives about 816 units, so the exact count varies by source. Wikipedia says the first phase opened in September 2016, and an architecture firm says seven builders built eight residential neighborhoods around a clubhouse called The Kindred House.

Miles of sidewalks and trails link the neighborhoods to the Tassajara Creek Regional Trail. These are community facts, so the plumbing in any one house depends on its builder, its phase and its owner history.

Plumbing questions for homes under ten years old

Read this section as general advice, since no public source reports on the pipes in this community. In a newer house, the first step for many problems is not a repair but a warranty check. Builders and fixture or appliance makers often give written warranties, and doing certain repairs yourself or with an outside company can affect them. Read your documents first, then ask before work starts.

Newer homes still leak. Supply line connectors, water heater fittings and shutoff valves can fail, and in a house with long pipe runs and finished walls, a small leak can hide for weeks. Leak detection is useful when a water bill climbs or a damp spot appears, and a water heater repair visit should start with the age and model label.

If you are finishing a basement, adding a bathroom or moving a laundry, plumbing installation needs a permit check with the City of Dublin, because new fixtures are not on its permit-exempt list. A dripping faucet is an easy early repair and often a cartridge or washer.

Keep a simple home file

Keep your closing documents, the builder's warranty and your appliance manuals in one place, and note where the main shutoff and water heater are. If a leak starts, photograph the area before cleanup, because builders and insurers may ask for it.

Ask the plumber to write down what they found and what they changed. That record helps if a warranty question comes up later.

What we could not verify

We found no public record of which builders used which pipe materials or water heaters in Wallis Ranch, so this page does not name any. Keep your own closing and warranty papers, because they are the authority on what your home has.

Codes and permits in Dublin

The California Plumbing Code, built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), applies to work in this neighborhood. The City of Dublin treats clearing a stoppage and repairing a leak differently from replacing fixtures or moving pipes, so ask which applies to you.

Questions from Wallis Ranch homeowners

Where is Wallis Ranch in Dublin?

A 2014 announcement places it north of Roxbury on Tassajara Road, extending uphill to the Alameda and Contra Costa county line. The city lists Wallis Ranch Park at 6701 Rutherford Road.

Should I call my builder before a plumber?

If your home is still under a builder or manufacturer warranty, yes, read the terms first. A plumber can still shut off water and prevent damage while you sort out coverage.

Does adding a bathroom need a permit in Dublin?

New fixtures are not on the City of Dublin permit-exempt list, so check with the city before work starts.
