Privacy Policy

How Dublin 24/7 Plumbing handles information when you call or visit this site.

Talk to dispatch now.(510) 544-1962.Call now.

This site has no forms and no accounts. We do not ask you to type personal details into the site.

Phone calls

When you call (510) 544-1962, we collect the information you give us, such as your name, address and the plumbing problem, so we can send a plumber. Calls may be recorded for quality and training purposes. By continuing a call after hearing the recording notice, you agree to the recording.

Website data

Like most sites, our host may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. We use this to keep the site running and secure. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share your details with the plumber assigned to your job and with service providers needed to complete it. We do not share them for unrelated marketing.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to contact@247plumberdublin.com.

Our Tri-Valley service area

Dublin sits at the center of the area we dispatch to, with Pleasanton, San Ramon, Livermore and Danville each within about 4 to 10 miles.
