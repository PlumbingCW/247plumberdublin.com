Established neighborhoods and what is behind the walls

Danville is a town, not a large city, and its charm comes from established neighborhoods with mature trees. Mature trees mean roots, and a sewer lateral that has been in the ground for decades is exactly where roots find a joint. A town with this profile calls for a camera early and a cable later, so we usually begin with sewer camera inspection on any recurring backup.

Houses that have been remodeled several times also hide plumbing history. A bathroom added in one decade and a kitchen moved in another leaves a mix of materials. When one fails we tell you what the neighboring lengths look like, and whether repiping belongs in the conversation.

Water heaters are another frequent reason to call. Many sit in garages or closets and may be a decade or more old. See water heater replacement for how we size and install.

If you have a burst pipe or an active leak in Danville

Start at the main valve, which is often on an exterior wall or in a garage in this part of Contra Costa County. Close it, then drain the lines at a low faucet. If you cannot find it, tell us, and we walk you through it on the phone.

Codes and permits in Danville

Plumbing work in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) from IAPMO. Permit rules are set by each city, so a job outside Dublin follows the local building department, and our plumber tells you before work begins if a permit applies.
