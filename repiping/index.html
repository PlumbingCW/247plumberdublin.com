Whole House Repiping in Dublin, CA

Written by the Dublin 24/7 Plumbing team. Updated November 16, 2026.

Whole house repiping in Dublin, CA means replacing the water supply lines in the house with new pipe, usually PEX or copper. It is worth considering when pinhole leaks keep appearing, when low pressure comes from corroded galvanized pipe, or when the home has polybutylene lines that are known to fail.

A repipe is not an emergency purchase, but a burst pipe often starts the conversation. If you have just had a leak, see our burst pipe repair page first, then come back to this page to plan the next step.

When repiping makes sense

One repair on one pipe is not a reason to replace everything. Repiping is worth pricing when there is a pattern: several leaks in a short time, rusty water from the hot or cold side, or low pressure that comes from interior pipe restriction rather than the regulator.

Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, narrowing the pipe until pressure drops. Polybutylene was widely installed in the late 1970s through the mid 1990s and has a history of failures at its fittings. Copper can develop pinholes. We identify the material in your home and give you an honest recommendation.

Pipe materials compared

Each material has a place, and the right choice depends on the house and the budget.

Material.Strengths.Watch for.
PEX.Flexible, fewer fittings, less wall demolition, resistant to corrosion.Not for outdoor exposure to sun, needs correct fittings.
Copper.Long track record, tolerates heat and sun.Higher cost, can pit with some water chemistries.
CPVC.Rigid plastic, lower cost.Can become brittle with age and heat.

Look for these before a major leak.

How a repipe works

We start by mapping the system: supply, branches, fixtures and the water heater. New lines are run through the attic, crawlspace or walls, depending on the house. PEX allows flexible routing and typically requires fewer wall openings than copper.

We open small access holes, run the new pipe, connect fixtures and test the system under pressure before closing. Drywall repair and painting are a separate trade, and we coordinate with your contractor or recommend one. Most whole-house repipes take several days, and water is off for part of each day.

If only part of the house has failing pipe, a partial repipe may make sense. We explain which sections we would replace and why.

Cast iron drain pipe and other pipe repair

Repiping usually refers to supply lines, but drain lines age too. Cast iron drain pipe, common in older homes, rusts and scales inside and can crack. Signs include slow drains in several fixtures, sewer gas smell and stains around pipe joints. A camera shows the condition.

Repairing a drain stack may involve cutting out sections and replacing them with plastic or no-hub cast iron. Our sewer line repair page covers the underground portion, and our drain cleaning page covers clearing.

Dublin homes and pipe age

Dublin had a few hundred residents in 1960 and nearly 14,000 at incorporation in 1982, per the City of Dublin, so housing runs from early suburban homes to newer construction. Homes from the 1960s and 1970s may have galvanized or copper, and homes built in the 1980s and early 1990s may include polybutylene. Newer homes generally have copper or PEX.

The age of your house is a clue, not a diagnosis. Past remodels may have changed the pipe. We look at what is in the walls. We also repipe homes in Danville and Pleasanton.

What to do before the crew arrives

Repiping opens walls, so a little preparation keeps the job smooth and safe.

Clear access

Move furniture and belongings away from walls where pipes run and from under sinks.

Plan for no water

Fill containers for drinking and flushing, and plan where to wash for the day.

Protect pets and children

Keep them away from work areas, dust and tools.

Know your shutoffs

Locate the main shutoff so you can find it later and show the plumber.

Codes and permits

The California Plumbing Code is built on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and it sets the baseline for repiping in Dublin. Clearing a stoppage or repairing a leak is on the City of Dublin permit-exempt list, while replacing fixtures or rearranging pipes is not, so ask us whether your job needs one.

Questions we hear in Dublin

How long does a whole house repipe take?

Several days for most homes, with water off for part of each day. It varies by house size and access.

Is PEX or copper better?

Both work. PEX is faster to install and flexible. Copper has a long track record. We explain trade-offs for your house.

Do I need a permit to repipe in Dublin?

Permit rules vary. Ask the City of Dublin Building and Safety Division. We can describe the work for your inspection.

Can I repipe part of the house?

Yes. If the failing pipe is isolated, a partial repipe can make sense. We tell you what we would replace.
