Water heater installation in Dublin, CA covers new tank installs, upgrades and moves, as opposed to an emergency swap. The planning matters because a heater touches the gas line or electrical circuit, the venting, the water supply and the drain, and each has to be right for the unit to run safely and last.

If your current heater has already failed, our water heater replacement page covers the same-day side. This page is for installs you can schedule, including moving a heater, converting fuel type or choosing a heat pump model.

What a proper install includes

A good installation starts with the site. We check the location for clearance, access for service, and a safe place to route the relief line. Gas units need a verified gas connection and venting that drafts correctly. Electric units need the right breaker and wire size for the element rating.

We connect new supply lines and shutoff valves, install a temperature and pressure relief valve with a discharge line to a safe location, and add an expansion tank when the system is closed by a check valve or a pressure regulator. Then we fill the tank, purge air, test for leaks, and light or power the unit.

The old heater is drained, disconnected and hauled away. Permit rules vary, so ask the City of Dublin Building and Safety Division what applies to your project, and we will work with your inspection if one is needed.

Heat pump (hybrid) water heaters

Heat pump water heaters move heat from the surrounding air into the tank, which is why they use much less electricity than a standard electric tank. They need open space and airflow, make a quiet fan noise, and cool the room slightly.

Federal and state incentives for heat pump water heaters have changed repeatedly. The federal Section 25C credit was scheduled to end for property placed in service after December 31, 2025, and California programs have had waitlists, so check current rules with the IRS, your utility and TECH Clean California before you buy. We can install the unit, and in some programs a participating contractor submits the incentive application for you.

Sizing, location and code details

Sizing depends on first-hour rating and household use. We ask the number of bathrooms, how many people shower in the morning, and whether the home has a soaker tub or a large kitchen. A tank that is too small runs out, and a tank that is too large wastes energy keeping water hot.

Location matters. Heaters in garages need protection from vehicle impact in some situations, and heaters in closets need combustion air. Seismic strapping is part of a proper install in California. Moving a heater to a new location adds supply, gas, venting and drain work, and we price that separately.

Because code details vary by project, ask the City of Dublin Building and Safety Division about permits, and we will describe the install in the terms they use.

Dublin water and your new heater

Dublin's water comes from the Dublin San Ramon Services District, supplied wholesale by Zone 7 Water Agency, which reports hardness above 300 mg/L in its groundwater and below 100 mg/L in its surface water. A new tank starts clean, but scale will begin to build. Flushing annually and checking the anode rod extends its life.

If hardness is a concern, see our water softener and filtration page. We install heaters across Dublin and in Pleasanton, San Ramon and Livermore.

Codes and permits

For water heater installation, the plumber works to the California Plumbing Code, which the state bases on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). If your project replaces or moves pipes, valves or fixtures, check with the City of Dublin before work starts, because that kind of work is not on its exempt list.

Questions we hear in Dublin

How long does a water heater installation take?

A like-for-like swap often takes a few hours. Moving a heater, changing fuel type or adding venting takes longer.

Do I need a permit in Dublin?

Permit rules vary by project, so ask the City of Dublin Building and Safety Division. We tell you what we will do and can work with an inspection.

What size water heater do I need?

It depends on household size and use. We look at first-hour rating, not just tank volume, and recommend a size.

Are there rebates for heat pump water heaters?

Programs change often. Check the IRS, your utility and TECH Clean California for current rules, and ask us about contractor-submitted incentives.
