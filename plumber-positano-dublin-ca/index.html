Where Positano is

Positano is an east Dublin subdivision. A City of Dublin page describes Sean Diamond Park at 4801 La Strada Drive as being in the Positano subdivision, and the city's parks map also lists Positano Hills Park. A real estate guide says Positano Parkway connects the neighborhood to the rest of Dublin and that it sits near I-580.

The city does not publish a boundary for the name, so we use La Strada Drive and Positano Parkway as anchors. Fallon Sports Park is nearby, and neighboring areas such as Dublin Ranch and Jordan Ranch are separate names.

What is documented about the homes

A real estate guide describes new traditional homes with stone exteriors and Spanish Colonial Revival details such as arched doorways and red tile roofs. It states no year built for Positano. The City of Dublin says Sean Diamond Park was dedicated on April 28, 2018, which dates the park but not the homes.

Because no source gives a build year, we do not claim one. Check your closing documents or permit history if you need the exact age of your plumbing.

Fixtures, disposals and hard water in newer homes

What follows is general plumbing knowledge, not a record of what is inside these houses. Newer homes tend to have modern fixtures and shorter pipe runs, which shifts calls toward fixtures and appliances more than pipes. Common examples are a dripping or low-flow faucet, a toilet that runs after flushing and a kitchen disposal that jams or hums.

Hard water ties these together. Zone 7 publishes hardness figures for its sources, and the district blend means scale is a regional issue. White crust on aerators, shower heads and heating elements is often the real reason a faucet or heater starts to underperform.

A garbage disposal repair usually starts with freeing a jammed flywheel, and a toilet repair usually starts with the fill valve or flapper. If scale is a repeating problem, water softener and filtration options are worth asking about, along with the salt, space and drain they need.

A quick check before the visit

A few minutes of checking can speed up the repair. Unscrew a faucet aerator and look for white grit, which points to scale. Run the disposal with cold water to see whether it hums or spins. Lift the toilet tank lid and see whether the flapper seats fully. If the cause is simple, the fix is fast.

Keep the model numbers of your fixtures handy. A plumber can then bring the right cartridge, flapper or fill valve on the first visit instead of making a second trip.

What we could not verify

We could not find what fixtures builders installed in Positano, or any test of water in these homes. The hardness figures are district-level system data and the plumbing guidance above is general, not a finding about any one house.

Codes and permits in Dublin

Plumbing work here follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) from IAPMO. The City of Dublin decides when a permit is needed, so ask before a larger job.

Questions from Positano homeowners

Where is Positano in Dublin?

The City of Dublin places Sean Diamond Park in the Positano subdivision, and a real estate guide links the area to Positano Parkway in east Dublin.

Why do my faucets and shower heads clog with white scale?

That is typical of hard water. Zone 7 reports high hardness in its groundwater, so scale on aerators and heating elements is a general expectation in this region.

When should I replace a garbage disposal instead of repairing it?

A jam is often fixable, but a leaking housing or a seized motor usually means replacement. A plumber can tell you which you have.
