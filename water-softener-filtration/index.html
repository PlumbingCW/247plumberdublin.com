Water softener and filtration installation in Dublin, CA starts with knowing your water. Dublin's water comes from the Dublin San Ramon Services District, which buys it wholesale from Zone 7 Water Agency, and Zone 7 reports that its groundwater is over 300 mg/L in hardness and its surface water is under 100 mg/L. The mix at your tap depends on the supply and location, so test your tap before choosing equipment.

A water softener removes hardness minerals, and a filter removes sediment, chlorine taste or other contaminants. They solve different problems, and many homes need one but not both.

Softener or filter: which one do you need?

Hardness shows up as white scale on faucets, spotted dishes, dry skin and shortened appliance life. A softener swaps calcium and magnesium for sodium or potassium through an ion-exchange resin. A filter removes particles and some chemicals but not hardness unless it is built to.

If your concern is taste or odor, a carbon filter or an under-sink reverse osmosis system may solve it. If your concern is scale, a softener or a scale-conditioning system is the tool. We ask what you are seeing before recommending equipment.

Each system targets a different problem.

What Zone 7 says about softeners

Zone 7 Water Agency says a softener can address the aesthetic quality of your water if you want to reduce hardness, and cautions that some softeners add sodium, so people on low-sodium diets should check with a doctor. It also says self-regenerating softeners are often discouraged because they add excess salt to wastewater.

Zone 7 gives hardness in mg/L and reports that western areas of its service territory are generally harder than eastern ones. We do not guess your number. Test your tap, or ask the Dublin San Ramon Services District for its annual water quality report, and we will use that to size a system.

Installation and maintenance

A whole-house softener installs on the main line, usually in a garage, with a drain connection for regeneration and an electrical outlet. Bypass valves let you service it. Installing it after the hose bib connections keeps untreated water for the yard.

We install, repair and maintain softeners and filters, including resin tank issues, control valve faults, salt bridging and regeneration problems. Water conditioner repair calls are common, and we diagnose the cause before replacing parts.

Dublin: what to ask before buying

Some dealers offer a free in-home water test as part of a sales visit. A hardness test strip costs a few dollars, and a lab test is more precise. Ask for the hardness number in grains per gallon or mg/L, and compare it to the district report.

The district may be able to tell you which supply serves your neighborhood. We serve Dublin, and nearby Pleasanton, Livermore and San Ramon, where water supply arrangements differ.

Questions we hear in Dublin

How hard is Dublin water?

Zone 7 reports groundwater over 300 mg/L and surface water under 100 mg/L, and the mix varies. Test your tap or ask the district for its report for your address.

Do I need a softener?

Only if hardness is causing problems you care about. A test and a look at your fixtures tell us.

Is a softener safe for drinking?

Some add sodium, and Zone 7 advises people on low-sodium diets to ask a doctor. Potassium options exist.

Do you repair softeners?

Yes. We diagnose control valves, resin tanks and regeneration problems.
