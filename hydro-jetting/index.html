Emergency hydro jetting in Dublin, CA uses a hose with a rotating nozzle and high-pressure water to scour grease, scale, sludge and roots from the inside of a drain or sewer line. Where a cable machine drills through the middle of a clog, a jetter cleans the full diameter of the pipe, which is why it solves recurring backups.

If your drains back up again within weeks of being cleared, ask for a camera look and a jetting pass. During an active backup, stop using water and call, because we need to clear the line before the pipe can be cleaned.

Emergency backup tonight: when jetting is the right call

A fully blocked main is an emergency, and our first move is usually a cable to restore flow. Jetting then cleans the grease and debris that caused it. For a restaurant, a multi-unit building or a house with a history of grease buildup, the jetter can be the faster path to a clean line.

If sewage is coming up, keep the area clear and avoid using any water. We arrive with a jetter or a cable and a camera, and decide the order after we see the line.

How hydro jetting works

A jetting hose carries water at high pressure to a nozzle with forward and rear-facing jets. The forward jets break through the clog, and the rear jets pull the hose along while scouring the wall. We choose a nozzle by pipe type: a spinning nozzle for grease, a chain-cutting or root nozzle for roots, and a gentler pattern for fragile pipe.

The pressure is set to the pipe. Healthy plastic and cast iron take a strong pass. Old clay, cracked pipe or pipe with offsets get a lower setting, or none at all, because water can force its way through damaged joints. A camera before jetting tells us which situation we are in.

After the pass we run the camera again, so you see the pipe wall before and after. If the footage shows a crack or sag, we discuss a repair and the next steps.

What jetting removes, and what it cannot fix

Jetting is cleaning, not repair. It is the right answer for several kinds of buildup.

Grease and soap

Hardened fat from kitchens and laundry, which cables leave behind.

Mineral scale

Deposits in older cast iron and galvanized drain lines.

Roots

Fine root growth inside joints, cut and flushed out. Roots return if the joint is open.

Not damage

A collapsed, cracked or offset pipe still needs repair after cleaning.

Camera first, camera after

We do not recommend jetting blind. A camera pass before the work tells us the pipe material, the depth, whether there are offsets or breaks, and where the debris sits. Jetting a broken line can make a small problem worse.

The follow-up camera pass is a record. It shows a clean wall, any defects revealed after cleaning, and the actual condition of the line. Our sewer camera inspection explains what the footage means and why it matters when you buy or sell a house.

Restaurants, multi-unit buildings and homes in Dublin

Commercial kitchens in the shopping areas along Dublin Boulevard and near the BART stations produce a steady stream of grease, so scheduled jetting is a maintenance item for many of them. We cover this on our commercial plumbing page.

For homes, jetting makes the most sense when the line has been cabled more than once, when the house is older and has cast iron, or when a camera shows scale and grease. The Dublin San Ramon Services District handles wastewater treatment for Dublin, and the property owner is generally responsible for the lateral to the district connection, so ask the district where responsibility changes.

If the line is already clear and flowing, you do not need jetting. We will say so.

Questions we hear in Dublin

Do you offer hydro jetting emergencies at night or on weekends in Dublin?

Yes. Our line is staffed 24/7, and we send equipment suited to the backup. The first step is often a cable to restore flow, followed by jetting.

Will hydro jetting damage my pipes?

On sound pipe it does not. On cracked, collapsed or very old clay pipe it can, which is why we run a camera first and set the pressure to the pipe.

How often should a line be jetted?

It depends on use. A home with a clean line may never need it. A restaurant line may need jetting on a schedule. A camera tells us how fast buildup returns.

Does jetting remove tree roots permanently?

It cuts and flushes roots, but they regrow if the joint they entered is still open. A liner or a point repair addresses the cause.
